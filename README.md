# Code Samples – Marcela

Hi, I'm Marcela 👋. I hold a master's in Economics, Data, and Policy from Central European University, and I aspire to conduct research and data analysis at the intersection of economics, public policy, and data science. I enjoy turning messy real-world questions into clear empirical projects 🤓. Some of my main research interests include:

- Development economics
- Competitive firm behaviour and market design
- Environmental Economics
- Forecasting and time-series analysis

## About this repository

This is a curated collection of data analysis, econometrics, forecasting, and geospatial projects. The notebooks and scripts illustrate methods in causal inference, time-series forecasting, machine learning, and spatial analysis. They are applied to economic activity, firm growth, prices, and environmental outcomes.

## Topics and methods

Across these projects, you will find examples of some of my projects pertaining to:

- **Causal inference**
  - Difference-in-differences and event-study designs  
  - Fixed-effects panel models  
  - Visualization of treatment effects and dynamics

- **Forecasting and machine learning**
  - Time-series models (ARIMA, exponential smoothing, etc.)

  - Integrated empirical strategy from data cleaning to estimation and robustness checks
